My Business Day — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

My Business Day is a business management application developed by Artur Pohosian. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use the application.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, the application may process:

  • Account information, including your email address and authentication identifiers.
  • Business information, including company names, industry categories and preferences.
  • Information you enter about clients, orders, tasks, appointments and financial records.
  • Subscription information and purchase status.
  • Technical information necessary for authentication, synchronization, security and application operation.
  • Notification-related information when you enable reminders or notifications.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide business management features.
  • Synchronize information across devices.
  • Manage subscriptions and access to Pro features.
  • Support reminders and notifications.
  • Maintain application security and reliability.
  • Respond to support requests.

3. Service Providers

My Business Day uses third-party services that support application functionality, including:

  • Supabase: account authentication, database storage and synchronization.
  • RevenueCat: subscription management and purchase entitlement verification.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: processing purchases and subscriptions through the respective platform.

These providers process relevant information according to their applicable privacy terms and our service configuration.

4. Business Data

Information you enter into the application is used to provide your business management workspace. You are responsible for ensuring that you have an appropriate legal basis to enter and manage personal information about your clients or other individuals.

5. Data Storage and Security

Application data may be stored on your device and synchronized through cloud services. We use technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained as needed to provide application functionality, maintain security, resolve issues and comply with applicable legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of information and its purpose.

7. Data Access and Deletion

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

To request account and associated data deletion, contact:

mybusinessday.support@gmail.com

Please identify the email address associated with your My Business Day account. Do not send your password.

8. International Data Processing

Information may be processed in countries other than your country of residence through the infrastructure of our service providers, subject to applicable data protection requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

My Business Day is designed for business and productivity purposes and is not specifically directed at children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the application, service providers or legal requirements. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
